Repair Service Terms

Plain-English terms for TCM repairs, cloning, data recovery, shipping and warranty work supplied by Kovac Services Ltd.

1. Service scope

We inspect the unit and carry out the service requested or otherwise agreed with you. A TCM repair covers the confirmed fault and work described, not every possible fault in the gearbox, mechatronic, wiring or vehicle.

2. Testing and no repair, no fee

Initial bench testing is free for the advertised supported repair services. If we cannot complete the supported repair, there is no repair charge. You remain responsible for carriage and any return-shipping costs. Prior repair damage, severe corrosion, physical damage or a different fault may require a revised quotation before work continues.

3. Prices and turnaround

DQ200 Standard repair is £179.99 with a typical turnaround of 2 to 3 working days from receipt. Same-Day Priority is £228.99 and targets test, repair and dispatch on the same working day when received before 12:00 noon and the unit has the confirmed supported fault without additional complications. Walk-in while-you-wait DQ200 repair is £249 by appointment. Courier transit time is separate from workshop turnaround.

4. Warranty

Completed DQ200 electronics repairs carry a 2-year unlimited-mileage repair warranty from the date of completion. The warranty covers recurrence of the fault and work we carried out. It does not cover unrelated hydraulic or mechanical faults, vehicle-side electrical faults, water ingress, physical or transit damage, incorrect installation, skipped or incorrect basic settings/adaptation, donor hardware failure, or later third-party work. Warranty carriage remains the customer's responsibility unless we agree otherwise in writing.

5. Cloning and data recovery

Cloning depends on donor compatibility and readable original data. Data recovery depends on the condition of the original module and cannot be guaranteed before inspection. Donor units and unrelated hardware are not warranted simply because data has been cloned to them.

6. Shipping and customs

You are responsible for packing the unit securely, selecting the courier and completing customs declarations accurately. We are not responsible for courier loss, damage or delay while the parcel is outside our custody. International customers remain responsible for their own export, re-import and courier requirements. Our customs support form is guidance and does not replace official customs documentation.

7. Payment and abandoned units

Payment is due when a successful chargeable service is confirmed, unless a trade account or other arrangement has been agreed. If a unit is left uncollected or return arrangements are not provided, we will make reasonable attempts to contact you before taking further action.

8. Consumer rights

Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable consumer law.
