Privacy Notice

How Kovac Services Ltd handles information submitted through tcmrepair.co.uk.

What we collect

When you contact us we may collect your name, telephone number, email address, vehicle details, TCM part number, fault information, country and any information you choose to include in your message.

Why we use it

We use enquiry and repair information to respond to you, assess the requested service, carry out the repair, arrange return, keep repair and warranty records, prevent fraud and meet legal or accounting obligations.

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand general website usage. Google may receive technical information such as device, browser and approximate location information according to its own privacy terms and your consent or browser settings where applicable.

Forms

Website enquiry forms are processed through Formspree. Information submitted in a form is transmitted to that service so it can be delivered to Kovac Services Ltd.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers where necessary to operate the website, process an enquiry, perform the service, arrange shipping or comply with law.

Retention and your rights

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including repair, warranty, accounting and legal records. UK data-protection law gives you rights over your personal information. To ask what we hold, request correction or make another data request, contact info@kslimited.co.uk.
