Repair philosophy

We repair the failed pressure-loss point.

Common DQ200 hydraulic failures can involve the accumulator sealing area, pressure storage, the accumulator mounting/housing, the pump system or internal leakage. We inspect before choosing the repair route rather than publishing the internal repair recipe.

  • Complete mechatronic accepted for hydraulic faults
  • Free test before repair charge
  • Quality repair components selected for the actual fault
  • Pressure build and retention checked after repair
Do not send TCU only

Hydraulic codes need the complete mechatronic.

The electronic control module on its own cannot reproduce the hydraulic leak or pressure-storage problem. For P17BF/P189C/P1895, send the complete mechatronic unless we tell you otherwise.
