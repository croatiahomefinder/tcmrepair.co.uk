Quality matters

The part inside matters.

A cheap repair can look identical from the outside. For supported pressure-sensor work we are building around traceable, recognised transmission components such as LuK/Schaeffler, rather than anonymous clone parts. DQ200 hydraulic repairs are matched to the actual failure rather than blindly replacing a complete mechatronic.
