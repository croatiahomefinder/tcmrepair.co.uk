DQ380 / DQ381 Bosch TCM

P1735 or P1736? Only odd or even gears?

Good news. This is exactly one of the repairs we specialise in. The Bosch DQ380/DQ381 TCM develops a repeatable clutch pressure-sensing fault that can leave one side of the gearbox unavailable, trigger emergency mode and temporarily recover after a restart.

Natural-language symptoms

This is what the fault sounds like before anyone knows the code.

These phrases are deliberately here because they are how owners and garages search when the transmission suddenly loses half its gears.

“My DSG only has even gears.”
“Reverse, 2nd, 4th and 6th still work.”
“It only has odd gears after the gearbox warning.”
“Gearbox emergency mode, then it works again after restarting.”
“P1735 / P1736 keeps coming back.”
“The fault is worse cold / after short trips.”

What we confirm before repair

  • Exact Bosch TCM hardware and gearbox family
  • Stored P1735/P1736/P0805 fault identity
  • Pressure-sensing values with the gearbox at rest
  • No evidence that the reported problem is actually an auxiliary pump or mechanical clutch fault
  • Bench function after repair before return
What you send

TCM / mechatronic electronics as instructed after free confirmation.

Send the label and fault codes first if you are unsure. DQ380/DQ381 hardware revisions matter, so we identify the controller rather than choosing parts from the gearbox name alone.

Different fault

P0C29 is not this repair.

An auxiliary hydraulic-pump fault can require vehicle/gearbox diagnosis. Do not remove and post the TCM for P0C29 unless we have reviewed the scan first.

ODIS + aftermarket scanners

Whatever your scanner calls it, we want you to recognise the fault.

ODIS often shows a VAG symptom/DFCC number alongside the P-code. VCDS and aftermarket tools may use a different English translation or only show the P-code.

10666 · P1735 · P173500 · P1735 00Position sender for clutch 1, electric fault / Position sensor for clutch 1, electrical malfunction
10668 · P1736 · P173600 · P1736 00Position sender for clutch 2, electric fault / Position sensor for clutch 2, electrical malfunction
P0805 · P080500Clutch Position Sensor Circuit / Clutch Position Sensor A Circuit

Important: on DQ380/DQ381 the diagnostic wording says “position sensor”, but the clutch position is calculated from the hydraulic pressure model. We do not tell customers to replace a generic clutch-position sensor based only on the text description.
